# Privacy Policy

**Status: DRAFT — requires UK solicitor review before publication.**
**Scope: covers the free Candidacy Scan only. Paid Preview/Unlock additions are flagged inline.**

_Last updated: 2026-05-05_

## 1. Who we are

Candidacy ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Silvia Jiang, a sole trader based in London, United Kingdom, contact candidacyuk@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the personal data described below.

## 2. What we collect

When you use the Candidacy Scan we collect:

- **Email address** — only if you choose to have your Scan result sent to you. Submitting the form is your action, not a default.
- **Scan answers** — the responses you give in the readiness questionnaire (route(s), evidence categories, career stage, narrative inputs). These are processed to generate your result.
- **Aggregate usage data** — page views and event counts (e.g. Scan started, Scan completed) via Plausible Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not collect personal identifiers, full IP addresses, or cross-site tracking data.

We do not collect: name, address, phone number, payment details, government identifiers, or any sensitive personal data.

_Paid Preview/Unlock (not yet launched): when introduced, we will additionally collect intake responses and process payments via Stripe. This policy will be updated before that launch._

## 3. Why we collect it (lawful basis)

| Data | Purpose | Lawful basis (UK GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Sending you the Scan report you requested | Performance of a contract / your request (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Scan answers | Generating the readiness assessment | Performance of a contract / your request (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Aggregate usage data | Understanding which channels bring users and where they drop off | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — improving the service |

## 4. How long we keep it

- **Email + Scan answers:** retained for 12 months from submission, then deleted, unless you request earlier deletion.
- **Aggregate usage data:** retained indefinitely in anonymised form; cannot be linked back to you.

## 5. Who we share it with (data processors)

- **Resend** (email delivery, EU region) — to deliver your Scan report email.
- **Netlify** (hosting and serverless functions) — to host the site and process the email-send request.
- **Plausible Analytics** (EU-hosted, cookieless) — for aggregate usage data.

We do not sell your data, do not add you to a mailing list, do not share with marketing partners, and do not use it to train AI models.

## 6. International transfers

Our processors store data within the UK or EEA. Where any processor transfers data outside the UK, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or equivalent safeguards.

## 7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access your data; correct it; erase it; restrict processing; object to processing; data portability; and lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk).

To exercise any of these rights, email candidacyuk@gmail.com. We respond within one month.

## 8. Cookies

The Candidacy Scan does not use tracking cookies. Plausible Analytics is cookieless. Strictly necessary cookies may be used for the language toggle and session state — these do not require consent under UK PECR.

## 9. Security

We use HTTPS for all connections, restrict access to data on a need-to-know basis, and review processor agreements annually. We will notify affected users and the ICO of any qualifying personal data breach within 72 hours of awareness.

## 10. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date. Where required, we will notify users by email.

## 11. Contact

candidacyuk@gmail.com — for any privacy question, request, or complaint.
